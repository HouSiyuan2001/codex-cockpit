# 🛸 Codex Cockpit

### Let your AI get to work. Keep your bearings.

What's left of your quota? How much did you use today? Which task ate the most tokens?<br>
A quiet little window keeps those answers close by.

[Download for Mac / Windows](https://github.com/HouSiyuan2001/codex-cockpit/releases/latest) · [Take a look](https://codex-cockpit.pages.dev/) · [中文](../README.md)

## Your AI's little sidekick 🐾

Stay focused while Codex works on your code, papers or projects. Cockpit lives at the edge of your screen; open it when you want to see your usage and plan what comes next.

- **Less quota guesswork.** Remaining quota, reset time and your daily plan, together.
- **Find the token-hungry tasks.** Explore usage and estimated costs by task or model, with a calendar heatmap.
- **Print a tiny receipt.** Select tasks and copy their totals as a receipt-style PNG.
- **Find your rhythm.** Plans, reminders and daily comfort records help you understand your own pace.
- **Start solo, share later.** Local tracking needs no new account or server. Optional sync brings trusted teammates or your other computers together.

This is an independent community project, not an OpenAI product. Website previews use sample data.

## Bring it home

Download the [latest release](https://github.com/HouSiyuan2001/codex-cockpit/releases/latest): choose `universal.dmg` for Apple Silicon / Intel Macs, or `x64-setup.exe` for Windows x64. Install, open, and start with local tracking.

Updates are checked in Settings; you confirm before restarting to install.

> The Mac build is not Apple-notarized; the Windows installer has no publisher signature. Verify the source and checksums. Do not disable system security. Costs are estimates, not provider bills.
